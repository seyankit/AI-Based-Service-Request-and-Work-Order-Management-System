ABOUT THE PROPOSED SYSTEM

A centralized approach to school service management.

The proposed AI-Based Service Request and Work Order Management System is designed to help Holy Trinity College organize service requests, assignments, work orders, status updates, and completion records in one centralized platform.

AI-assisted features may help authorized personnel classify requests, recommend priority levels, and identify possible duplicate concerns. Final decisions remain with authorized school personnel.
