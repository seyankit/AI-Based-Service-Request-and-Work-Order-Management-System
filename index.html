Role-Based System

Each user sees only the interface assigned to their role

The public page is shared by everyone. After sign-in, the system opens the correct sub-interface and keeps all other role workspaces hidden.

01

Requester

Submit requests and track only their service records.

02

Service Administrator

Review requests, create work orders, and manage access.

03

Department Head

Review requests that require authorization and approve or reject them.

04

Technician

View assigned work and update progress or completion.

Responsible AI Assistance

AI supports decisions without replacing authorized personnel.

The AI component reviews request descriptions to recommend a category and priority and to flag possible duplicate concerns. Final approval and work assignment remain with authorized users.

✓

Human review remains required. Administrators validate AI suggestions and the Department Head or designated approver makes the authorization decision.

01

Classification

Recommends the most likely service category.

02

Priority

Estimates urgency based on the reported impact.

03

Duplicate Check

Flags similar active requests for human review.

04

Service Insights

Supports monitoring, dashboards, and reports.

Service Workflow

From request to verified resolution

Each stage has a responsible role and a documented system record.

  1. 01 Requester

    Submit

    Enter a concern and receive a request number.

  2. 02 AI + Administrator

    Analyze

    Recommend and validate category, priority, and duplicates.

  3. 03 Department Head

    Approve

    Authorize or reject requests requiring approval.

  4. 04 Administrator

    Assign

    Create the work order and assign service personnel.

  5. 05 Technician

    Resolve

    Perform the work and record progress or completion.
